## 2. Professional Positioning & Domains
Er. Sadhuram Lamichhane works at the intersection of land surveying, earth observation, spatial computation, and infrastructure planning. The website directly reflects and honors these core specializations:

- **Geographic Information Systems (GIS)**: Advanced spatial modeling, multi-criteria decision analysis, spatial databases (PostGIS/PostgreSQL), web GIS, geoprocessing automation.
- **Geomatics Engineering**: Cadastral survey, land administration, geodetic networks, coordinate reference systems, precision measurement.
- **Remote Sensing & Earth Observation**: Satellite imagery analysis, multispectral/optical sensor processing, indices calculation (NDVI, NDWI, etc.), land-cover classification.
- **UAV / Drone Surveying & Photogrammetry**: Flight planning, aerial survey, high-resolution orthophoto generation, 3D point cloud reconstruction.
- **LiDAR & Topographic Surveying**: Digital Elevation Models (DEM), Digital Terrain Models (DTM), contour derivation, volumetric calculations, slope/aspect analysis.
- **GNSS & Geodetic Survey**: Static, RTK, and PPK differential GPS positioning, ground control point (GCP) establishment, network adjustment.
- **Hazard & Resilience Mapping**: Landslide susceptibility modeling, flood inundation mapping, seismic risk assessment, climate vulnerability analysis.
- **Infrastructure & Land-Use Planning**: Municipal road alignments, hydropower catchment delineation, urban zoning, master planning, watershed management.
- **Research & Academic Teaching**: Field training, university instruction, GIS capacity building for government engineers, published or ongoing academic research.
