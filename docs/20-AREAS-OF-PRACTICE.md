## 1. Purpose
Section 04 provides a calm, typographic discipline overview following the immersive horizontal motion of Section 03. It establishes the full technical breadth of Er. Lamichhane's professional practice without repeating the visual 01–04 sequence in Section 02.

## 2. Six Core Disciplines
1. **01 / GIS & Spatial Analysis**: Enterprise geodatabase architecture, spatial multi-criteria evaluation (MCE/AHP), and digital municipal profiling.
2. **02 / Land Use Planning & Zoning**: Statutory parcel-level (kitta-wise) classification and zoning compliance under Nepal's Land Use Act 2076 & Regulation 2079.
3. **03 / Geodetic Surveying & GNSS**: High-order geodetic control networks, static/kinematic GNSS baseline processing, and detailed engineering route surveys.
4. **04 / Remote Sensing & Photogrammetry**: Multi-temporal satellite image interpretation, land use/land cover (LULC) transition detection, and optical spectral analysis.
5. **05 / UAV & LiDAR Mapping**: Low-altitude drone surveys, dense 3D point cloud generation, high-resolution DSM/DTM extraction, and slope cut/fill calculation.
6. **06 / Hazard & Disaster Resilience Mapping**: Terrain slope stability modeling, hydrological catchment delineation, and infrastructure vulnerability assessment.
