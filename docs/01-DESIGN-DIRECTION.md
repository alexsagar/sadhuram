# Design Direction: Cartographic Neutral / Engineering Editorial

## 1. Visual Direction Philosophy
The visual direction is **Cartographic Neutral / Engineering Editorial**.

The portfolio exists at the intersection of:
- **Engineering Precision**: Clean coordinate systems, measured lines, authentic cartographic conventions, geometric exactness.
- **Scientific Clarity**: High legibility, objective hierarchy, data-first structuring, absence of rhetorical fluff.
- **Editorial Composition**: Asymmetric breathing room, purposeful typographic cadence, balanced column structures, and fine print craft.
- **Geospatial Visual Language**: Abstracted terrain elevation, elevation contours, parcel boundaries, survey benchmark markers, analytical zones.
- **Real Project Evidence**: Authentic cartographic outputs, orthomosaics, georeferenced maps, and technical reports rather than generic tech illustrations.
- **Controlled Motion**: Deliberate, smooth, physical spatial transitions rather than flashy UI tricks.
- **Restraint as Authority**: Every element exists because it carries information. Empty space is treated as structural margin, not an area to fill with decorative gadgets.

---

## 2. What it Feels Like
- A master-level engineering publication.
- An engineering drawing, survey document and cartographic paper publication.
- A prestigious earth-sciences or architectural research portfolio.
- Calm, authoritative, deliberate, mathematically grounded, and culturally rooted in Himalayan/Nepali topography and infrastructure.

---

## 3. What it Must NOT Look Like
- **NOT an AI-generated landing page**: No glowing purple orbs, no floating glass cards, no generic isometric graphics.
- **NOT a SaaS template**: No pricing grids, no feature badges with pill tags, no marketing slogans, no fake testimonial stars.
- **NOT a crypto / gaming site**: No neon matrix grids, no dark cyber-HUD interfaces, no particle vortexes.
- **NOT a generic web developer portfolio**: No cartoon avatars, no "Hi, I'm..." generic introductions, no tech-stack icon walls (React/Node badges).
- **NOT software UI recreation**: Do NOT recreate ArcGIS, QGIS, or AutoCAD software interfaces. We abstract spatial concepts into an editorial experience.

---

## 4. Visual Inspiration Sources
- **Topographic & Hypsometric Maps**: Natural earth curves, elevation gradients, elevation profiles, index contours.
- **Cadastral & Parcel Survey Plans**: Orthogonal bounds, coordinate ticks, benchmark indicators, traverse lines.
- **Digital Elevation Models (DEM / DTM)**: Hillshade surfaces, shaded relief, tri-mesh surfaces, grid cells.
- **Cartographic Atlases & Swiss Editorial Design**: Strict baseline grids, generous margins, restrained palettes, clear technical legends.
- **Himalayan Landscape Morphology**: Steep elevation shifts, river watersheds, complex valley profiles, geological resilience.

## 5. Approved palette correction
